# Quantifying the causal impact of a marketing campaign with ML

I was asked to analyse the impact of a new advertising campaign on business sales. The catch: there was no control group.

Rather than relying on a simple before/after comparison and assuming the results, I developed a causal inference machine learning model (with Bayesian Structural Time Series), using my domain expertise to carefully select impactful and relevant covariates to boost model performance.

The result: a statistically significant quantification of the campaign's causal impact, enabling business leaders to confidently adjust budgets and spend allocation to maximise ROI.
